# Crit 10 reflection

## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

I think the biggest breakthrough this week was the redesign. I did it last week,
but it was for this week: the complete UI redesign, and fixing the game logic
into what it is right now.

The logic fix came first, and it came from playing the game rather than from
the tests. In the first version the mole copied one friend's taste, and every
test passed, but when I played it the mole was easy to find: the person being
copied knew their own pick, so the copy stood out. Switching to a hidden theme,
where the mole has a slightly different prompt and doesn't know it, is what made
it a real game. The redesign then made it something I'd actually hand to my
friends: the zine look, instructions on every screen, characters at a table,
and a past-games page.

## What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

Actually playtesting the game, and making sure it is user-friendly and
user-centric, is the biggest thing. I got confused playing my own game, and a
second game at the same table locked everyone except the mole out of picking.
Neither showed up in a test. The 3D table looked fine with four players and hid
the wheelchair users with six. I want to be a developer who checks how it feels
to use, not just whether it passes.
