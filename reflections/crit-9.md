# Crit 9 reflection

## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

I think the biggest breakthrough for this crit, since there weren't a lot of big
issues, was updating the harness and adding the new rules about orchestrating and
double-checking to it. Aside from that, one big thing was writing the tests for
the game before it existed: some hard tests to keep everything in check.

## What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

It definitely made me think about how harnesses are different from prompts, and
how interesting they are.
