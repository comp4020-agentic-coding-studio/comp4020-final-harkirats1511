Mole Records

Mockups for the final project, nothing built yet. The app as a record shop after hours: the home page is the shopfront, the hub is the shop floor you walk around, tables are listening booths, your theme is a staff-pick card, tracks are white-label records, and the reveal is the liner notes on the back cover.

Try it: tap the shop floor in screen 2 to walk there. In screen 4, tap a sleeve to drop that record on the turntable.

4. Listen: the needle drop. The record slides out of its sleeve, lifts onto the platter, the tonearm swings over and the needle drops, then it spins. The label shows only side and track, like a white-label pressing, because a title would give the owner away. Reduced motion: it skips straight to the record on the platter.
